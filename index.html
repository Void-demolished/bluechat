How to Use BlueChat
💬
Sending a message
Click the input bar at the bottom, type your message, and press Enter.
👤
Changing your username
Click the username box in the header, type your new name, and press Enter or click away. Your name must be unique in the channel you're in.
#
Joining a channel
Type a channel name or number in the Channel box and press Enter. Share the same channel name with a friend to chat together. You start in channel 1.
↩
Replying to a message
Right-click any message bubble to reply to it. A bar appears above the input showing who you're replying to — press ✕ or Escape to cancel.
⚙ Quick Toggles
🔒
Safe Mode
Flip this on to make the tab look like Google — handy if you need to hide the page quickly.
🔵
Blue Input Bar
Turn this on if you want the message bar to glow blue whenever you click into it.
